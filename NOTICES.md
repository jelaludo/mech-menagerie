## Methods and measurements

- The gi's proportions follow senshu-jerseys by John Morris (MIT), https://github.com/johnmorrisdotca/senshu-jerseys.
- The skin-weight transfer follows Abdrashitov, Raichstat, Monsen and Hill, "Robust Skin Weights Transfer via Weight
  Inpainting" (SIGGRAPH Asia 2023 Technical Communications).
