QA verdict: SHIP WITH NOTES

The gate of docs/QA.md (adapted from the arjun988/blender-skills QA, LOD and budget skills, MIT). No blockers. Majors: 121k triangles against a 50k to 100k hero budget (LOD1 at 61k is the in-game candidate); a few pants points through the front flaps at the waist in the rolls (white on white, up to 6 cm); H-01's neck parts through the back of the collar for a moment in the wave (up to 2 cm); and against the reference sculpt the shoulders and back read bulky and lumpy. The verdict in full is in the model's README.

The belt's tails (after the user's note on the walk)

"Only a bit of clipping of the pieces going down while walking": the belt's tails. Solved smoothly down from the knot, they lagged the skirt they lie on, and its front swung through them; now they copy the skirt's weights below the hip. And the collar band running down the front edge came through the tail lying across it; it is now removed under the belt. Tails sinking into the skirt in the walk, jog and run: 1 to 2 points (8 to 12 mm) before, none now (numbers).

A second host: the Red Witness

The same gi, drafted afresh for the Red Witness (rigged on the kit for it, with the action showcase's motions, which were recorded on this figure). In the viewer: Red Witness in a Gi. Not an easier body after all: its forearms hang 4 cm from its thighs, so it holds its arms 10 degrees out; its barrel chest makes the jacket boxy. Nothing of the body comes through in any clip but the back roll (2 points, probably the check); pants through the jacket 1 to 3 points in the gaits, behind two layers; 98,657 triangles (inside the hero budget). The verdict: its README (SHIP WITH NOTES); the story and the numbers: the study, section 13 (motion check).

H-01 in the gi: the drape

The jacket rebuilt the way a gi is cut: the shape measured from photographs (senshu-jerseys), straight sleeves set on along a slit in the body's side, the skirt cut and draped over the pants' envelope, and a solid collar band and belt. Third iteration: a closer fit (3 cm of ease a side, not 6), the lapels crossing part way up the armhole and held there by an inside tie, the collar one row up the neck, soft pins.

Pants: built, then draped

Top: built closed round the body with the legs spread 12°. Bottom: after the drape (legs back to rest, drawstring cinched). Stretch worst 1.31×; body poking through 3 points, worst 2.6 mm; 20 s.

Jacket: the drape over time

Frame 1: built closed with the arms raised 25° (pants not shown: the jacket drapes over their envelope). Frames 20 to 70: the belt cinches, the arms come down to 8° open. Frame 110: settled. Collar pinned at the neck, belt rows pinned at the waist.

Collision bodies for three hosts

The smooth stand-in the cloth lies on, in blue over each model: H-01, the gorilla G-02, the toad. Body outside it: 1.1%, 1.5%, 3.0%.

The spike: sewing against building closed

Top row: flat panels sewn shut from 0.8 m apart (never closed, crumpled). Bottom row: the same panels built closed round the body (seams welded, 1% stretch). Frames 1, 10, 25, 90.
